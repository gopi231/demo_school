Twelve years of getting students into the college they wanted

Zenith Learning Academy was founded in 2014 by a group of IIT and AIIMS alumni who wanted coaching that felt more like mentorship than a lecture hall. We keep batches small on purpose — every student is known by name, not roll number.

We run programmes for Class 9–10 foundation, Class 11–12 with Board integration, and dedicated JEE and NEET dropper batches.
